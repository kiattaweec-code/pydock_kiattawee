Prepare Dock

1 · Load a complex

A structure straight from the PDB is not a receptor: the bound ligand is still in it, and pydock would treat it as part of the protein.

2 · Choose the ligand

Click a molecule in the list, or click it in the viewer.

    3 · Confirm the chemistry

    A PDB file records no bond orders. Paste the ligand's SMILES and they are taken from it, so the MOL2 is exactly the compound you name. Leave it empty and pydock works them out from the geometry, which fails on larger ligands.

    4 · Clean up

    Recommended. pydock adds polar hydrogens itself, and it only rotates hydroxyls when the file arrives without any.

    Structural metals are usually part of the site and are scored.

    5 · Results

    Send to docking

    The prepared receptor and ligand stay on the server, so docking picks them up without a download.
