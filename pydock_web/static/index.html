Dock a ligand in the browser

      
Upload a receptor and one or more ligands, say where the binding site
        is, and the engine searches ligand conformation, position and
        orientation with a genetic algorithm — rotating receptor
        hydroxyls along the way.

      
    
        
  • Flexible ligand. Every rotatable bond is a search
          variable, built into a torsion tree.
    • 
        
  • Cavity detection. The search is confined to the
          detected pocket rather than the whole box, which is worth more than
          any other single setting.
    • 
        
  • Two scoring functions. Switchable per run, with a
          per-term breakdown reported for every pose.
    • 
        
  • See the box before you run. Upload a receptor and
          the pockets are detected for you; drag the docking box into place
          and watch it move in 3D.
    • 
        
  • Screen a library. Drop in an SDF with many
          molecules, or several files, and every one is docked and ranked.
    •
